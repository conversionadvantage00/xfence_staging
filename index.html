Questions

Asked before most quotes.

What is Clear View fencing?

Clear View fencing is made from welded steel mesh panels with small openings. It gives you a strong boundary that you, your cameras and your guards can still see through, where a solid wall would block the view.

Which mesh aperture should I choose?

The 76 × 50 mm mesh is the most open and suits homes, complexes and general perimeters. The 76 × 25 mm mesh is tighter and harder to grip. The 76 × 12.7 mm mesh is our tightest, for industrial and high-risk sites. If you are unsure, we will help you choose during a free consultation.

What sizes do the panels come in?

Every panel is 3 050 mm long, in heights of 1 800, 2 100, 2 400 and 3 000 mm. All three apertures use 4 mm horizontal and vertical wires and come in a galvanized finish.

Can it be installed on uneven ground?

Yes. Clear View fencing can be installed on uneven terrain. Tell us about any slopes when you ask for a quote, so we can allow for them in the panel count.

Can it support electric fencing?

Yes. Clear View fencing can support electric fencing. Let us know if you plan to add it when you ask for a quote.

How do I get a price?

Use the quote builder on this page, send us a WhatsApp or call the office. Tell us the fence length, height and aperture and we will confirm the panel count and send you a quote. The consultation is free.
